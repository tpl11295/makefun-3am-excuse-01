Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-hazel-walrus-38-4764-the-3-am-excuse-c1017878) bounty **"The 3 AM Excuse"** — one line, written in the voice of a hardware wallet refusing to sign a transaction at 3 AM.

> 3 AM is when my secure element sleeps and your worst decisions wake up — I refuse to co-sign your insomnia. Come back at sunrise, when your judgment does too.

Run `node main.ts` — it writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.
